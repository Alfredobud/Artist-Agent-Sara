# Weekly Workflow

## Monday

- Review leads.
- Define the week's priority.
- Plan content.
- Review Sarah's schedule.

## Tuesday

- Create scripts.
- Create captions.
- Prepare posts.
- Review videos.

## Wednesday

- Prospect planners, venues, photographers, and DJs.
- Send relationship-building messages.

## Thursday

- Follow up with leads.
- Prepare proposals.
- Review responses.

## Friday

- Analyze metrics.
- Adjust content.
- Update CRM.
- Plan next week's recordings.

## Estimated Time

- First 30 days: **8 to 10 hours per week**
- Days 31 to 90: **6 to 8 hours per week**
- After 90 days: **up to 6 hours per week**
