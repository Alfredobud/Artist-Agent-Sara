# Operating Process

## Lead Flow

1. Lead submits website form or sends social media DM.
2. Sales Agent sends the initial response and requests missing event details.
3. Lead is entered into the CRM with status **New Lead** or **Contacted**.
4. Sarah's team confirms date, location, event type, guest count, desired moments, and budget range.
5. Sales Agent recommends a package.
6. Proposal Agent creates a personalized proposal.
7. Follow-up is scheduled within 2 to 4 business days.
8. Once accepted, the event moves to agreement, deposit, and planning call.

## Content Flow

1. Review weekly event schedule and rehearsal opportunities.
2. Select three short-form content ideas.
3. Record clean audio/video clips.
4. Use Content Creator Agent for captions and hooks.
5. Publish across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
6. Track performance in the metrics document.

## Partnership Flow

1. Market Research Agent identifies planners, venues, photographers, and DJs.
2. Send planner or venue outreach template.
3. Track responses and relationship notes in CRM or a separate partner sheet.
4. Share video links and package overview.
5. Follow up monthly with useful content, not only sales messages.
