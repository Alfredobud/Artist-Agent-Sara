# Service Packages

## Core Positioning

Sarah is positioned as a live singer and band-front artist for wedding receptions, post-wedding celebrations, private parties, and corporate events. The main value is not a short ceremonial solo moment; it is creating energy, connection, and a memorable party atmosphere through live vocals, cover songs, and band performance.

## Package 1 — Wedding Reception Band Experience

A live reception-focused performance designed to keep guests engaged after the formal wedding moments and throughout the celebration.

**Ideal for:** couples who want the party after the wedding to feel alive, elegant, and memorable.

**Includes:**

- Live vocals with band or band-style setup
- Curated cover songs for a wedding audience
- Danceable and emotional repertoire
- Reception timeline coordination
- Setlist planning based on couple preferences
- Optional first dance or special dedication song

## Package 2 — Cocktail Hour + Reception

A smooth musical transition from post-ceremony cocktail hour into a more energetic reception atmosphere.

**Ideal for:** couples who want live music to welcome guests, then build energy for the celebration.

**Includes:**

- Cocktail hour live music
- Reception performance set
- Elegant background moments plus upbeat party songs
- Romantic, pop, soul, classics, Latin, acoustic, and dance-friendly covers
- Coordination with planner, DJ, venue, or event lead

## Package 3 — Full Celebration Experience

A complete entertainment experience for couples who want Sarah and the band to shape the emotional and energetic flow of the event.

**Ideal for:** weddings and celebrations where live music is a central part of the guest experience.

**Includes:**

- Cocktail hour support
- Reception performance sets
- First dance or featured couple moment
- Party-building repertoire
- Custom song planning
- DJ/planner coordination
- Guidance for transitions, announcements, and special moments

## Package 4 — Private & Corporate Band Events

A flexible live entertainment package for private parties, corporate gatherings, hotels, lounges, holiday parties, and social celebrations.

**Ideal for:** hosts and organizers who want a professional live singer and band atmosphere for guests.

**Includes:**

- Private dinners and family celebrations
- Corporate receptions and holiday events
- Hotels, lounges, and special event venues
- Curated cover setlists
- Background-to-party energy planning
- Professional coordination with event organizers
