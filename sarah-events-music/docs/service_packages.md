# Service Packages

## Package 1 — Ceremony Signature

A focused wedding ceremony experience for couples who want meaningful live vocals during the most emotional moments.

**Ideal for:** intimate and traditional ceremonies.

**Includes:**

- Bride entrance
- Signing or unity moment
- Couple exit
- 2 to 4 songs
- Planning meeting
- Personalized repertoire selection

## Package 2 — Ceremony + Cocktail Hour

A complete ceremony package with an elegant live music atmosphere as guests transition into celebration.

**Ideal for:** couples who want continuity from ceremony to guest experience.

**Includes:**

- Full ceremony music
- 60 to 90 minutes of cocktail hour
- Romantic, classic, pop, soul, or acoustic repertoire
- Coordination with planner or event lead

## Package 3 — Full Event Experience

A premium music experience that supports ceremony, cocktail hour, first dance, and selected reception moments.

**Ideal for:** couples who want a personalized musical journey throughout the day.

**Includes:**

- Ceremony
- Cocktail hour
- First dance
- Partial reception support
- Personalized music planning
- DJ/planner coordination
- Soundtrack experience guidance

## Package 4 — Private & Corporate Events

A flexible package for future expansion into private dinners, family celebrations, hotels, lounges, and corporate gatherings.

**Ideal for:** hosts and organizers who want elevated live vocals without building a full entertainment production.

**Includes:**

- Private dinners
- Family parties
- Corporate events
- Celebrations
- Hotels and lounges
- Holiday events
