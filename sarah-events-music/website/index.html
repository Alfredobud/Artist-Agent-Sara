About Sarah

Live vocals and band energy for celebrations your guests will remember.

Sarah is a Charlotte-based singer and band-front performer bringing warmth, presence, and energy to wedding receptions, private parties, and special events. With live performance experience in local venues, she is now shaping a professional event entertainment experience for couples, planners, families, companies, and hosts who want more than background music.

Her work is centered on the celebration: cocktail hour, reception, first dance, party sets, cover songs guests recognize, and the moments where a live singer and band can keep the room connected and energized.

FAQ

Planning questions

Do you perform only in Charlotte?

Sarah is based in Charlotte and serves the surrounding metro area, including Lake Norman, Huntersville, Cornelius, Mooresville, Matthews, Ballantyne, Concord, and Fort Mill. Travel can be discussed for select receptions, private parties, and corporate events.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Personalized song selection is part of the planning process, especially for first dance moments, dedications, favorite covers, and the overall party setlist.

Do you bring sound equipment?

Sound needs depend on the venue, band format, and package. Sarah’s team will confirm what is available onsite and what needs to be arranged.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, inquire as early as possible once your date and venue are confirmed. Popular dates can book months in advance.

Do you perform with a band, backing tracks, or acoustic setup?

Sarah can discuss the best format based on the event size, venue, song choices, and desired energy.

Can you coordinate with our wedding planner or DJ?

Yes. Coordination helps transitions feel smooth and reduces event-day stress.

How do we secure the date?

The date is secured after package confirmation, agreement, and deposit.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a deposit is recommended to reserve the date, with remaining balance due according to the agreement.
