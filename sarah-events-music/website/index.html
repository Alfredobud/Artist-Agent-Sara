About Sarah

Elegant live vocals for moments your guests will remember.

Sarah is a Charlotte-based vocalist bringing warmth, elegance, and emotional presence to weddings and special events. With live performance experience in local venues, she is now shaping a more personalized event music experience for couples, planners, families, and hosts who want more than background music.

Her work is centered on the most meaningful parts of the day: the walk down the aisle, the first song guests hear at cocktail hour, the first dance, and the intimate moments that deserve a live, human voice.

FAQ

Planning questions

Do you perform only in Charlotte?

Sarah is based in Charlotte and serves the surrounding metro area, including Lake Norman, Huntersville, Cornelius, Mooresville, Matthews, Ballantyne, Concord, and Fort Mill. Travel can be discussed for select events.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Personalized song selection is part of the planning process, especially for ceremony entrances, exits, and first dance moments.

Do you bring sound equipment?

Sound needs depend on the venue and package. Sarah’s team will confirm what is available onsite and what needs to be arranged.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, inquire as early as possible once your date and venue are confirmed. Popular dates can book months in advance.

Do you perform with backing tracks or acoustic setup?

Sarah can discuss the best setup based on the song choices, venue, and desired atmosphere.

Can you coordinate with our wedding planner or DJ?

Yes. Coordination helps transitions feel smooth and reduces event-day stress.

How do we secure the date?

The date is secured after package confirmation, agreement, and deposit.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a deposit is recommended to reserve the date, with remaining balance due according to the agreement.
