# Lead Statuses

- **New Lead:** Inquiry received and not yet answered.
- **Contacted:** Initial response sent.
- **Waiting Reply:** Sarah's team is waiting for client details or confirmation.
- **Proposal Sent:** Custom proposal delivered.
- **Follow-up Needed:** Next action is overdue or scheduled soon.
- **Closed Won:** Event booked and deposit/contract completed.
- **Closed Lost:** Client chose another option or canceled.
- **Not a Fit:** Event does not match Sarah's service, location, budget, or availability.
